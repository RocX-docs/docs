Active Energy, or AE, is a non-transferable offchain point that represents eligible activity value inside RocX.

It can support participation across DeFi Planet, PoA Planet, and Explore Planet, but it is not a token and does not guarantee conversion, distribution, payment, yield, or future financial value.

<Info>
Activity registration creates a record for review.

Qualified activity can become eligible for Active Energy.
</Info>

AE and Reputation have different roles.

Active Energy is a usable participation resource connected to eligible activity. Reputation is a non-transferable record of long-term trust, contribution quality, consistency, and policy compliance.

Holding more AE does not automatically create higher Reputation.

The exact AE amount, timing, campaign policy, ranking policy, and evaluator reward are not fixed in this whitepaper.
