RocX evaluates usefulness, not emotional popularity. Eligible community signals are interpreted with contributor verification, content validity, duplication checks, and abuse review.

<Info>
Helpful adds a positive signal. Not Helpful adds a negative signal.
</Info>

## Helpful and Not Helpful

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Helpful" icon="thumbs-up">
    The activity provides useful Web3 information, education, analysis, insight, or practical value.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Not Helpful" icon="thumbs-down">
    The activity lacks sufficient informational, educational, analytical, or practical value.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Not Helpful does not automatically remove an activity. Helpful or Not Helpful does not guarantee factual accuracy, legality, safety, or investment suitability.

## Evaluation eligibility

Users must maintain at least **USD 50 in eligible RocX DeFi deposits** to evaluate activities. The initial threshold may be adjusted through operating policy.

- One eligible wallet can evaluate each activity once.
- Helpful and Not Helpful cannot be selected at the same time for one activity.
- Deposit size does not increase evaluation count or weight.
- **1 eligible wallet = 1 evaluation per activity.**
- Evaluations linked to abuse may be excluded.

## Daily evaluation limits

| Evaluation | Daily maximum |
|---|---:|
| Helpful | 10 |
| Not Helpful | 10 |
| Combined possible actions | 20 |

The Helpful and Not Helpful limits are counted separately. The daily reset follows RocX's service operating timezone as defined in operating policy.

## Raw Activity Score

```text
Raw Activity Score = Eligible Helpful - Eligible Not Helpful
```

The score does not add all reactions together. Only eligible evaluations count, and Not Helpful contributes a negative signal.

## Contribution review

Contribution Review can consider:

- original source and contributor ownership;
- supported platform and Web3 relevance;
- uniqueness, accessibility, and continuing validity;
- eligible Helpful and Not Helpful results;
- policy compliance and invalidation; and
- automation, related accounts, reciprocal behavior, or other abuse signals.

```text
Final Eligible Score = Raw Activity Score adjusted by verification and abuse review
```

## Contributor Season Score

The Contributor Ranking aggregates eligible scores from each contributor's verified activities.

```text
Contributor Season Score = Sum of eligible activity scores from the contributor's verified activities during the current ranking round
```

The ranking is contributor-based rather than a single-post ranking. Verified links and eligible evaluations count; hidden, invalidated, duplicate, policy-violating, or abuse-linked activity may be excluded.
