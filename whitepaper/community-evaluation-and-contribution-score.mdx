Meaningful contribution cannot be measured by raw likes alone.

RocX combines eligible community evaluation with verification, quality, relevance, consistency, Reputation, and abuse review.

<Info>
Evaluation is a signal.

Contribution Score is an interpretation of multiple signals.
</Info>

---

## Helpful is not a popularity vote.

Eligible users can evaluate whether registered activity provides informational, educational, or practical value to the Web3 ecosystem.

The available signals are **Helpful** and **Not Helpful**. They evaluate the usefulness of an activity, not personal sentiment toward its contributor.

One eligible wallet can provide one evaluation per activity. The same wallet cannot provide Helpful and Not Helpful at the same time, and a larger deposit does not create more votes or greater voting power.

Whether an evaluation can be changed and how self-evaluation is restricted remain operating-policy decisions.

---

## Not Helpful is different from Report.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Not Helpful" icon="thumbs-down">
    A quality signal about the informational, educational, or practical value of the activity.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Report" icon="flag">
    A separate path for phishing, impersonation, illegal or harmful content, and policy violations.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Not Helpful does not automatically remove an activity. Visibility and enforcement require separate review and policy logic.

---

## Evaluation requires economic responsibility.

Evaluators must meet a minimum eligible deposit requirement. An initial USD 50 threshold is under consideration and can be adjusted through operating policy.

The requirement is intended to increase the cost of abuse, reduce automated or careless reactions, and connect RocX's Capital Layer with its Participation Layer.

This whitepaper does not decide whether registration requires a deposit, how long a deposit must remain, whether net or gross deposits apply, when eligibility is measured, or what happens after withdrawal.

---

## Contribution Score combines multiple signals.

Contribution Score may consider verified Helpful and Not Helpful evaluations, unique evaluators, originality, Web3 relevance, content validity, consistency, contributor Reputation, repeated contribution, reports, policy violations, related accounts, reciprocal evaluation, and other manipulation signals.

The exact formula and weights are not fixed.

High raw engagement does not guarantee a higher score. Manipulated signals can be excluded or reduce the contribution assessment.

<Warning>
Contribution Score does not guarantee Active Energy, ranking, payment, token distribution, or financial return.
</Warning>
