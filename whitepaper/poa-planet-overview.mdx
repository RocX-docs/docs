The Participation and Contribution Layer of RocX

PoA Planet is a cross-platform activity layer where users register Web3 contributions, have them verified and evaluated, and connect meaningful participation to Active Energy, Reputation, and Activity Identity.

<Info>
Create anywhere. Prove it on RocX.
</Info>

---

## Web3 activity happens everywhere.

Research, tutorials, analysis, education, development, community content, protocol usage, and ecosystem participation happen across many platforms.

PoA Planet connects supported activity into one contribution history without moving the original content away from its source.

Users can register original links from platforms such as X, YouTube, Reddit, TikTok, Facebook, Threads, Instagram, Telegram, Medium, Mirror, GitHub, and other sources approved through operating policy.

The supported platform set can expand and should not be treated as permanent.

---

## Registration begins with the original link.

Users submit the original content URL.

They do not write or upload a title, body, image, video, attachment, or custom thumbnail inside RocX.

When supported and appropriate, RocX can retrieve source metadata such as the platform, original contributor, title or excerpt, publication time, preview image, content type, and original URL.

If metadata cannot be retrieved safely or should not be displayed, PoA Planet can show limited source information and a link to the original content.

---

## Contribution moves through verification and evaluation.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Register activity">
    The user submits the original URL for review.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify the source">
    RocX checks source support, accessibility, platform, and content validity.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify the contributor">
    RocX checks the relationship between the registrant and the original contributor through supported methods.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review uniqueness and relevance">
    Duplicate, repeated, inaccessible, irrelevant, malicious, spam, and policy-violating activity can be excluded.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Evaluate contribution">
    Eligible participants provide Helpful or Not Helpful quality signals that are interpreted with other data.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect qualified value">
    Qualified activity can become eligible for Active Energy and contribute to Reputation and Activity Identity.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Activity states and transitions follow the implemented product specification. This whitepaper does not fix their exact names.

---

## The Activity Board makes contribution visible.

PoA Planet can organize verified activity through an Activity Feed, platform filters, recent or contribution-based sorting, Popular Activity, My Activity, Bookmark, contributor recognition, and My AE Balance.

Raw likes do not determine contribution value or ranking. Contribution Score can combine community evaluation, originality, relevance, validity, consistency, Reputation, policy compliance, and abuse signals.

Bookmarking is a discovery feature and is not an Active Energy event by itself.

---

## PoA Planet is not another social network.

RocX does not replace or host the platforms where Web3 content is created.

It registers original external links, verifies contributors and activity, evaluates contribution through eligible participants, and connects cross-platform records to the RocX value layer.

Internal comments, direct messages, and follower relationships are not defined as part of this whitepaper structure.

---

## Contribution creates more than one outcome.

PoA Planet is not a separate social product and DeFi is not the only destination of PoA value.

Verified contribution can connect to Active Energy, Reputation, Activity Identity, contributor recognition, partner opportunities, Explore participation, ecosystem access, and better DeFi experiences.

Partner projects may later use PoA Planet for research, tutorials, ecosystem updates, education, event reviews, product feedback, and community content campaigns. Pricing, campaign costs, reward funding, and revenue models are not fixed.
