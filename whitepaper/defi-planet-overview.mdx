DeFi Planet is the **Capital Layer** of RocX. It provides supported onchain financial activity and the capital context used across the broader activity system.

<Info>
DeFi Planet measures capital.

PoA Planet measures contribution.

Explore Planet keeps participation moving.
</Info>

## Current product scope

The documented DeFi product scope uses the following names:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Deposit" icon="vault">
    Supply supported assets to an available RocX market or vault.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Borrow" icon="hand-holding-dollar">
    Borrow supported assets against eligible collateral under displayed market rules.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Repay" icon="arrow-rotate-left">
    Reduce or close an outstanding supported borrow position.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Withdraw" icon="money-bill-transfer">
    Withdraw eligible supplied assets subject to available liquidity and position requirements.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Swap" icon="arrows-rotate">
    Exchange supported assets through the available product route.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Bridge" icon="bridge">
    Move supported assets across available networks.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Availability, networks, assets, collateral factors, rates, fees, and transaction conditions should follow the implemented product interface and guide.

Governance, Staking, and Radar are not presented as current RocX MVP functions in this whitepaper. Governance participation may still appear as an external Web3 activity example when it occurs in another supported ecosystem.

---

## Capital connects with contribution

Supported wallet and DeFi records can contribute to the Capital Graph. PoA Planet adds verified contribution, evaluation, and Reputation context without treating contribution as a deposit feature.

Eligible deposit requirements may support economically responsible evaluation, but registration eligibility and evaluation eligibility remain separate policies.

---

## DeFi is core, not exclusive

DeFi is RocX's core financial layer, but it is not the only outcome of PoA value. Qualified contribution can also connect to Active Energy, Reputation, Activity Identity, Contributor Recognition, partner opportunities, Explore participation, and ecosystem access.
