AE Boost is an umbrella term for policy mechanisms that may adjust Active Energy in defined product contexts. It must not be confused with how RocX recognizes the quality of PoA contribution.

---

## Deposit AE Boost

Deposit AE Boost can refer to product rules that adjust AE connected to eligible deposit activity.

Possible inputs may include:

- eligible deposit activity;
- consecutive attendance or participation conditions; and
- supported NFT Profile conditions.

Exact assets, thresholds, multipliers, periods, attendance rules, NFT eligibility, and caps belong in the implemented product policy and user guide. This whitepaper does not fix those values.

---

## Contribution Recognition

Contribution Recognition evaluates PoA activity through a different set of signals, which may include:

- contribution quality and Contribution Score;
- source, contributor, and verification results;
- Reputation and consistency;
- relevance and policy compliance; and
- abuse and manipulation review.

Contribution Recognition may support Reputation, Activity Identity, Contributor Recognition, ecosystem access, partner opportunities, Explore participation, and AE eligibility where policy allows.

---

## Separate policies, explicit connections

High-quality PoA contribution does not automatically increase a Deposit AE Boost multiplier. A product specification may connect the two only when that relationship is explicitly defined.

Likewise, a deposit-based boost does not prove contribution quality or create higher Reputation by itself.

<Warning>
AE Boost does not promise a fixed multiplier, reward, payment, token allocation, or financial return.
</Warning>
