Trust accumulated through verified participation

Reputation is a non-transferable record of long-term trust and contribution inside RocX.

It is not created by one activity, one popularity number, or one Active Energy balance.

<Info>
Active Energy is a participation resource.

Reputation is a long-term record.
</Info>

---

## Reputation grows from multiple signals.

Reputation can reflect verified activity count, contribution quality, consistency, areas of expertise, eligible community evaluation, valid-content ratio, policy compliance, negative activity history, and repeated ecosystem participation.

No single signal automatically determines Reputation.

Raw Helpful counts, deposit size, or AE balance do not create Reputation by themselves.

---

## Cross-platform contribution can build trust.

PoA Planet connects verified external content contribution to a record that can persist across supported platforms.

Research, tutorials, analysis, education, development, feedback, and community contribution can become part of that record when the source, contributor, relevance, validity, and quality can be trusted.

Manipulated, invalid, inaccessible, or policy-violating activity can be excluded or reduce the strength of the record.

---

## Reputation supports Activity Identity.

Reputation helps explain what a user has built over time.

It can connect wallet activity, cross-platform contribution, community evaluation, consistency, and areas of expertise into Activity Identity.

Reputation is not transferable and should not be presented as an immediately tradable score or guaranteed financial access.

---

## Trust can create broader opportunity.

Reputation can support contributor recognition, partner opportunities, ecosystem access, Explore participation, and better DeFi experiences.

The exact benefits and eligibility rules can evolve through product and operating policy.

<Info>
Trust is not claimed.

It is built through verified contribution over time.
</Info>
