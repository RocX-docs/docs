## From external contribution to RocX value

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create or perform activity">
    Users create onchain capital activity or meaningful Web3 contribution across supported external platforms and ecosystems.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Register the original link">
    For cross-platform content, users submit the original URL rather than uploading the content to RocX.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify the activity">
    RocX reviews the source, contributor relationship, platform, uniqueness, Web3 relevance, validity, and abuse signals.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Evaluate contribution">
    Eligible wallets can provide one Helpful or Not Helpful quality signal per activity without deposit-weighted voting power.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Interpret multiple signals">
    Contribution Score can combine evaluation, originality, relevance, validity, consistency, Reputation, and abuse review without a fixed public formula.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect qualified value">
    Qualified activity can become eligible for Active Energy and contribute to Reputation, Activity Identity, recognition, access, Explore participation, and better DeFi experiences.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## Registration, evaluation, and rewards are separate.

Registering an activity creates a record for verification.

Meeting an eligible deposit requirement can qualify a user to evaluate activity. It does not automatically define registration eligibility or reward entitlement.

Active Energy depends on applicable quality, campaign, period, policy, and abuse-review criteria. No fixed reward follows from one link or one Helpful signal.
