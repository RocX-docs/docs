RocX connects external contribution and onchain capital activity through a staged value process. The path varies by activity type; not every step applies in the same way.

---

## The value path

<Steps>
  <Step title="External or Onchain Activity">
    A user creates supported Web3 contribution on an external platform or performs supported onchain activity.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Registration">
    External content is registered through its original URL. Onchain activity may be observed from supported wallet and protocol records without a content submission.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Source and Contributor Verification">
    Where applicable, RocX reviews the source, platform, accessibility, contributor relationship, and available account connection.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Community Evaluation Where Applicable">
    Eligible Evaluators may provide Helpful or Not Helpful signals for contribution types that use community evaluation. Some activity types may use other verification paths.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Contribution and Abuse Review">
    Originality, relevance, validity, policy compliance, duplicate behavior, related accounts, reciprocal evaluation, automation, and other manipulation signals may be reviewed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Eligibility">
    The activity is checked against the quality, campaign, period, product, and operating rules that apply to that activity type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Contributor Ranking for PoA Content">
    Eligible verified content scores are aggregated by contributor, reviewed for abuse, and finalized for the current round.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Active Energy and/or Reputation">
    The finalized PoA ranking may distribute AE from the PoA Rewards Pool to the top 100 eligible contributors. Reputation may develop separately over time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Activity Identity">
    Valid capital activity, contribution, evaluation, consistency, Reputation, and AE history can accumulate as long-term context.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ecosystem Opportunities">
    Qualified records may support Contributor Recognition, partner opportunities, Explore participation, ecosystem access, and better DeFi experiences.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## Outcomes remain separate

- Community Evaluation is used where the activity type and operating policy require it.
- Verification does not guarantee Active Energy.
- Active Energy and Reputation do not have to be created at the same time.
- Activity Identity is a long-term accumulated signal, not the result of one action.
- A previous-round score does not automatically carry into the next round.
- Better DeFi can be one outcome, while verified contribution can also create recognition, identity, ecosystem access, partner opportunity, and continued participation.
