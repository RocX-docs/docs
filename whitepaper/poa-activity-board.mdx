The PoA Activity Board is the product surface where cross-platform Web3 contribution becomes visible and usable inside RocX.

It helps users register original links, discover verified activity, review their own contribution history, and understand how activity connects to Active Energy and Reputation.

<Info>
The Activity Board organizes contribution.

It does not host the original content.
</Info>

---

## Discover verified activity.

The Activity Board can provide an activity feed with platform filters and recent or contribution-based sorting.

Popular Activity can highlight contributions that perform strongly during a period, but it is not determined by raw likes alone. Verification, Contribution Score, Reputation, quality, and abuse signals can all affect visibility.

---

## Register and manage activity.

My Activity can show registered links, source platforms, retrieved source metadata, registration history, verification or visibility status, Helpful and Not Helpful signals, eligible Active Energy, and Reputation impact.

Exact status names and management actions follow the implemented product specification. This whitepaper does not fix edit, hide, delete, or invalidation behavior.

## The Activity Board is not a social graph.

The Activity Board does not define internal comments, replies, direct messages, or follower relationships.

Its purpose is to connect external contribution to verification, evaluation, Active Energy, Reputation, Activity Identity, partner opportunities, Explore participation, ecosystem access, and better DeFi experiences.
