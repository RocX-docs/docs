The PoA Activity Board makes verified cross-platform Web3 contribution visible inside RocX without hosting the original content.

## Activity Feed

The feed can show verified activity cards with platform filters, recent ordering, contribution-based popularity, **Helpful**, **Not Helpful**, **Bookmark**, and **View Original Activity**.

When source metadata is available, a card may show the platform, author or channel, external title or excerpt, publication time, thumbnail, video duration, content type, and original URL. If safe metadata is unavailable, the card may use the platform, author, registration time, original URL, and View Original Activity action.

## Popular Activity

Popular Activity highlights content that receives strong eligible evaluation over an applicable period. It is not based only on views, raw likes, or total reactions. Verification, net eligible evaluation, validity, and abuse review can affect visibility.

## Contributor Ranking

Contributor Ranking shows the current user ranking, percentile, current-round score, and reward eligibility. It aggregates the user's eligible verified activities rather than ranking only one post.

## My Activity

My Activity may show:

- registered original links and platforms;
- external title or other retrieved metadata;
- registration time;
- conceptual verification and visibility status;
- Helpful, Not Helpful, and net score;
- current-round inclusion;
- AE distribution history; and
- Reputation impact information.

## Bookmark and My AE Balance

Bookmark lets users save useful external activities. Bookmark count is not part of the AE reward formula by itself.

My AE Balance may show current AE, PoA ranking reward history, and other AE ledger changes. PoA AE is distributed through finalized Contributor Ranking, not immediately when a link is registered or verified.

## Not a social graph

The Activity Board does not add internal comments, replies, direct messages, content uploads, or follower relationships. RocX links external contribution to verification, eligible evaluation, Contributor Ranking, Active Energy, Reputation, and Activity Identity.
