RocX is a **Proof of Activity-powered DeFi platform** that combines onchain capital activity with verified user participation.

Capital, contribution, and reputation connect through one measurable value layer without reducing Web3 activity to a simple social mission or reward event.

---

## What is RocX?

RocX connects onchain financial activity and cross-platform Web3 contribution through **Proof of Activity**.

Traditional DeFi measures capital and liquidity. RocX adds verified participation, contribution quality, Reputation, and Activity Identity to that financial context.

<Info>
DeFi is RocX's Capital Layer. Proof of Activity connects that capital context with verified participation and contribution.
</Info>

## Why DeFi Stays at the Center

DeFi remains RocX's core financial layer.

PoA value is not limited to one deposit product or one financial benefit. Verified contribution can connect to Active Energy, Reputation, Activity Identity, contributor recognition, partner opportunities, Explore participation, ecosystem access, and better DeFi experiences.

<Note>
Capital is one part of value. Contribution and long-term trust are also part of the RocX experience.
</Note>
