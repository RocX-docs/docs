RocX is a **Proof of Activity-powered DeFi platform** that combines onchain capital activity with verified user participation.

Capital, contribution, and reputation connect through one measurable value layer without reducing Web3 activity to a social mission or one-time reward event.

RocX is also building a new activity-centered blockchain community where users, contributors, builders, and partners have reasons to return, participate, and grow together over time.

By connecting Web3 contribution created across both Web2 and Web3 platforms, RocX turns fragmented activity into a shared layer for recognition, retention, and new opportunity.

<Info>
Activity creates community. Community creates retention. Retention creates new opportunity.
</Info>

---

## What is RocX?

RocX connects onchain financial activity and cross-platform Web3 contribution through **Proof of Activity**.

Traditional DeFi measures capital and liquidity. RocX adds verified participation, contribution quality, Reputation, and Activity Identity to that financial context.

<Info>
DeFi is RocX's Capital Layer. Proof of Activity connects that capital context with verified participation and contribution.
</Info>

---

## From Web2 and Web3 Activity to Shared Opportunity

Many Web3 users already research, explain, build, and share through familiar Web2 platforms while also participating through wallets, protocols, and onchain communities.

RocX connects supported contribution from those environments without moving or claiming ownership of the original content. Verified activity can strengthen Reputation and Activity Identity, improve discovery, and connect users with ecosystems, partners, and better DeFi experiences.

As those records and opportunities accumulate, users gain stronger reasons to return and contribute again. This creates deeper community retention and a broader opportunity space for users, creators, builders, communities, and partners.

## Why DeFi Stays at the Center

DeFi remains RocX's core financial layer.

PoA value is not limited to one deposit product or one financial benefit. Verified contribution can connect to Active Energy, Reputation, Activity Identity, contributor recognition, partner opportunities, Explore participation, ecosystem access, and better DeFi experiences.

<Note>
Capital is one part of value. Contribution and long-term trust are also part of the RocX experience.
</Note>
