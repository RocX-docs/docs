There is no widely adopted shared standard that connects cross-platform Web3 contribution with onchain capital activity and financial context.

RocX uses the term **Proof of Activity Market** to describe the emerging category around that connection.

---

## A fragmented landscape

Social networks, quest platforms, creator programs, onchain identity systems, and DeFi loyalty products each recognize part of user activity. Their purpose, data model, and economic context differ.

The comparison below describes broad product categories. Individual services may use different designs.

| Category | Hosts original content | Registers external links | Contributor and quality signals | Capital and identity context |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Social networks | Usually | Not as a contribution standard | Accounts, followers, and engagement | Usually separate from DeFi capital activity |
| Quest platforms | Usually not | May verify links or actions | Task completion and campaign rules | Often campaign-specific |
| Creator reward platforms | Varies | Varies | Creator identity and program metrics | Financial context varies by program |
| Onchain identity and reputation systems | No | Attestations and external data may vary | Credentials, attestations, or reputation records | Strong identity context; contribution models vary |
| DeFi loyalty systems | No | Usually not content-focused | Protocol usage and loyalty rules | Strong capital context; cross-platform contribution is limited |
| RocX Cross-Platform Proof of Activity | No | Original URL registration | Contributor verification, Eligible Evaluators, Contribution Score, and abuse review | Capital Layer, Reputation, and Activity Identity |

---

## RocX's distinction

RocX does not compete by hosting another content feed. It connects supported external contribution to source and contributor verification, economically responsible evaluation, Contribution Score, Reputation, onchain capital context, and Activity Identity.

This category is still developing. Platform support, verification coverage, scoring policy, partner adoption, and product availability can evolve without changing the core direction.

<Info>
The opportunity is not to count more reactions.

It is to make meaningful contribution portable as verified context.
</Info>
