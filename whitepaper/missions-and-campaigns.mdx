Partner Campaigns are a future expansion path for PoA Planet. They can organize meaningful, verifiable Web3 contribution while preserving the current link registration, contributor verification, evaluation, and abuse-review principles.

Potential formats include:

- project research;
- product tutorials;
- educational videos;
- ecosystem updates;
- event reviews;
- product feedback;
- developer content; and
- other approved Web3 contribution campaigns.
