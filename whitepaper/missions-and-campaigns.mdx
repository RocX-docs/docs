title: "Partner Campaigns"
description: "A future expansion path for verified project research, education, feedback, and ecosystem contribution."
icon: "handshake"
---

Partner Campaigns are a future expansion path for PoA Planet. They can organize meaningful, verifiable Web3 contribution while preserving the current link registration, contributor verification, evaluation, and abuse-review principles.

Potential formats include:

- project research;
- product tutorials;
- educational videos;
- ecosystem updates;
- event reviews;
- product feedback;
- developer content; and
- other approved Web3 contribution campaigns.

Original content remains on the platform where it was created and is registered through its original URL. Legacy follow-based missions may remain in historical records, but they are no longer part of the current PoA Planet contribution model and are not presented as a future campaign format.

Campaign-specific AE budgets, partner fees, RocX fees, campaign rankings, the relationship to the primary Contributor Ranking, partner review, and campaign funding remain unconfirmed. The PoA Rewards Pool must not be assumed to fund every partner campaign.

<Warning>
A Partner Campaign does not guarantee AE, ranking, payment, token distribution, implementation, or launch timing.
</Warning>
