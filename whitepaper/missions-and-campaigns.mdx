Partner campaigns are a future expansion path for PoA Planet.

They can help projects organize verifiable Web3 contribution without reducing Proof of Activity to a simple follow mission or social quest.

<Info>
Partners can define contribution goals.

RocX can verify and connect qualified activity.
</Info>

---

## Campaigns can support meaningful contribution.

Potential campaign formats can include project research, product tutorials, ecosystem updates, educational content, event reviews, product feedback, and community content campaigns.

The original content can remain on the platform where it was created while the contribution is registered and verified through RocX.

A follow verification or other simple social action may exist as one supported task inside a verified campaign. It does not represent the full PoA model and does not automatically create Active Energy.

---

## Partners can discover contribution quality.

Partner campaigns can help projects discover verified Web3 contributors, identify high-quality content, review community response, segment users through activity signals, and recognize long-term contributors.

Contribution Score, Reputation, validation, and abuse review can provide more context than raw engagement counts.

---

## Campaign policy remains flexible.

The exact campaign eligibility, duration, supported platforms, contribution criteria, Active Energy policy, evaluation logic, and visibility rules can vary.

Pricing, campaign fees, reward funding, and partner revenue models are not fixed in this whitepaper.

<Warning>
A partner campaign does not guarantee reward, ranking, payment, or token distribution.
</Warning>
