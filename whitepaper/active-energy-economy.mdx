Active Energy, or AE, is a non-transferable offchain point recorded in the RocX ledger. It has no fixed cash value, cannot be immediately converted to a token, and has no fixed conversion rate to ROCX.

## AE sources

AE may arise through approved rules for:

- Daily Check-in;
- Deposit AE;
- Deposit Boost;
- PoA Ranking Rewards;
- Explore Results; and
- other approved activity.

Registering or verifying a PoA link does not create immediate AE. PoA AE is distributed after the Contributor Ranking, abuse review, and final ranking.

## AE uses

AE can be used for Explore mission participation and other approved ecosystem uses. Each use follows its applicable eligibility, settlement, expiry, and abuse rules.

## Two reserve accounts

| Reserve | Purpose | Initial source described here |
|---|---|---|
| **Active Energy Reserve Pool** | Common ecosystem AE reserve for approved settlement, inactivity deductions, and other approved reserve accounting | 5% of normally settled winning Explore result AE, plus other approved entries |
| **PoA Rewards Pool** | AE reserve dedicated to PoA Contributor Ranking distributions | 5% of normally settled winning Explore result AE |

The Active Energy Reserve Pool may appear as **Total AE Reserve Pool** in user-facing interfaces. The PoA Rewards Pool is a separate offchain ledger account. Neither reserve is a token treasury, cash fund, or onchain asset pool.

## Explore settlement

For a normally settled winning result in Roulette, Card Flip, Prediction, or another approved Explore mission:

| Gross result | User | Active Energy Reserve Pool | PoA Rewards Pool |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 100 AE | 90 AE | 5 AE | 5 AE |
| 100% | 90% | 5% | 5% |

```text
Explore Mission
→ Successful AE Settlement
→ 90% User
→ 5% Active Energy Reserve Pool
→ 5% PoA Rewards Pool
→ Contributor Ranking Rewards
```

This split applies only to normally settled winning results. Cancellation, refund, draw, or failed-settlement treatment follows the applicable mission specification.

Prediction and Card Flip are not described as using separately pre-funded pools. The PoA Rewards Pool does not fund game wins; it funds the PoA Contributor Ranking.

## Inactivity

Existing approved inactivity deductions may continue to be recorded under the Active Energy Reserve Pool. This whitepaper does not redirect inactivity deductions to the PoA Rewards Pool or invent a new inactivity formula.

## PoA distribution

The PoA Rewards Pool balance accumulated during a current ranking round is distributed to the top 100 eligible contributors under the initial 30% / 30% / 24% / 16% bands described in the current contributor-ranking policy.

The balance and per-contributor AE vary by round. The bands may be adjusted through operating policy and do not guarantee a fixed return.

<Warning>
AE is not fiat currency or a token. AE does not guarantee token allocation, payment, yield, financial return, or future value, and no fixed AE-to-ROCX conversion rate exists.
</Warning>
