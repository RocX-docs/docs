Explore Planet is RocX's **Engagement Layer**. It uses Active Energy in approved participation experiences and helps participation continue across the ecosystem.

<Info>
DeFi Planet measures capital. PoA Planet measures contribution. Explore Planet keeps participation moving.
</Info>

## A distinct role

PoA and Explore are different systems.

| PoA Planet | Explore Planet |
|---|---|
| Registers external Web3 contribution | Provides participation missions |
| Verifies source and contributor | Applies mission-specific AE rules |
| Uses eligible community evaluation | Includes Roulette, Card Flip, Prediction, Attendance, and approved formats |
| Builds Contributor Ranking and Reputation | Creates repeated engagement |

Explore does not replace PoA's contribution-verification role. An Explore result does not automatically create Reputation or a PoA score.

## Mission-specific mechanics

Each Explore mission defines its eligibility, entry, result, cancellation, refund, draw, failed-settlement, expiry, and abuse rules in the applicable product specification or guide. This whitepaper does not create a separately pre-funded pool for Prediction or Card Flip.

## Successful settlement

Each Explore mission keeps its approved payout math. When a mission's normally settled winning result includes the shared 10% allocation, 5% is recorded in the Active Energy Reserve Pool and 5% in the PoA Rewards Pool.

The PoA Rewards Pool is used only for Contributor Ranking distributions. It does not fund the Explore win itself.

```text
Explore Missions
→ Successful AE Settlement
→ 5% Active Energy Reserve Pool
→ 5% PoA Rewards Pool
→ Contributor Ranking Rewards
```

<Warning>
Do not apply one universal user-payout formula to every mission. Other outcomes follow each mission's approved specification.
</Warning>
