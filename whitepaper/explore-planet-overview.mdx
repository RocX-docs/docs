The Engagement Layer of RocX

Explore Planet is where Active Energy supports discovery, repeated participation, and ecosystem experiences.

It does not replace PoA Planet as the contribution and verification layer.

<Info>
Explore Planet keeps participation moving.
</Info>

---

## Engagement begins after value becomes usable.

DeFi Planet creates capital context.

PoA Planet registers, verifies, and evaluates contribution.

Explore Planet gives users ways to use or connect Active Energy to continued ecosystem participation.

---

Existing concepts such as card-based exploration, group or spin experiences, and market prediction can remain examples of Explore participation.

The exact mission set, eligibility, reward amount, return rate, removal rate, and reward-pool policy can change through product and operating policy.

---

## Explore and PoA have different responsibilities.

PoA Planet measures contribution and builds verified records.

Explore Planet creates engagement experiences that can use those records and Active Energy.

A partner mission or follow verification can exist as one supported activity type, but a simple social action does not define Proof of Activity.

---

## Participation can return to the ecosystem.

Explore experiences can be designed so a portion of activity value supports future participation or ecosystem balance.

This whitepaper does not fix a permanent percentage or distribution formula.
