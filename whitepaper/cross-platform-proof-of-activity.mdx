## Contribution begins outside RocX.

Users research, explain, build, share, and contribute across the broader Web3 ecosystem.

That work may appear on platforms such as X, YouTube, Reddit, TikTok, Facebook, Threads, Instagram, Telegram, Medium, Mirror, GitHub, and other sources supported through operating policy.

The supported platform set can expand over time. It is not a permanent or exhaustive list.

---

## The original content stays at its source.

RocX does not ask users to rewrite or upload the content inside PoA Planet.

Users register the original content URL. When supported and appropriate, RocX may retrieve source metadata such as the platform, original contributor, title or excerpt, publication time, preview image, content type, and original URL.

This information comes from the external source. It is not content authored or hosted by the user inside RocX.

If metadata cannot be retrieved safely or should not be displayed, RocX may show only limited source information and a link to the original content.

## Contribution becomes visible through verification.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create Web3 content">
    The contribution is created on an external platform.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Register the original link">
    The user submits the source URL to RocX.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify the source and contributor">
    RocX checks the source, contributor relationship, uniqueness, relevance, validity, and abuse signals.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Evaluate the contribution">
    Eligible participants can provide quality signals that are interpreted with other verification data.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record the activity">
    Qualified activity can become part of the user's contribution history.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build value over time">
    Eligible activity can connect to Active Energy, Reputation, and Activity Identity.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## RocX is not another social network.

Traditional social platforms host content and optimize for attention inside their own networks.

RocX does not replace the platforms where Web3 content is created. It provides the verification and value layer that connects those activities.

RocX does not introduce internal comments, direct messages, or follower relationships as part of this whitepaper structure.
