Registration creates a record for review.

It does not create an automatic reward.

PoA Planet verifies whether an external contribution can become a trusted activity signal inside RocX.

<Info>
A link becomes valuable only after its source, contributor, and activity can be trusted.
</Info>

---

## Registration begins with the original URL.

Users submit the original content URL rather than entering a title, body, image, video, attachment, or custom thumbnail inside RocX.

When supported, RocX can detect the platform and retrieve appropriate source metadata. The original platform remains the source of the content.

---

## Verification checks more than availability.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Source Verification" icon="link">
    Checks whether the URL is accessible and comes from a supported or approved source.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Contributor Verification" icon="user-check">
    Checks the relationship between the registrant and the original contributor through connected accounts or other supported methods.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Platform Detection" icon="globe">
    Identifies the original platform from the submitted URL when possible.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Uniqueness Verification" icon="copy">
    Detects duplicate links, repeated submissions, and modified URLs that point to the same content.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Web3 Relevance" icon="cubes">
    Reviews whether the contribution relates to blockchain, crypto, DeFi, infrastructure, communities, or connected ecosystems.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Content Validity" icon="badge-check">
    Reviews deleted, private, inaccessible, malicious, spam, or policy-violating content.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Abuse Review" icon="shield">
    Reviews multi-wallet behavior, reciprocal evaluation, automation, related account groups, and other manipulation signals.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

---

## Contributor verification starts with original contribution.

Users may connect supported external accounts to their RocX account and wallet. When the original contributor matches a connected account, the activity can be recognized as the user's own contribution.

The exact method can vary by platform and operating policy.

Whether third-party curation is supported, how it is credited, and how Telegram channels or groups identify contributors remain policy decisions. This whitepaper does not finalize those rules.

---

## Verification states remain a product policy.

An activity may move through review, approval, rejection, visibility, or later invalidation states.

The exact names and transitions are not fixed in this whitepaper. They should follow the implemented product specification and operating policy.

<Warning>
Registration does not guarantee verification, Active Energy, ranking, or any other benefit.
</Warning>
