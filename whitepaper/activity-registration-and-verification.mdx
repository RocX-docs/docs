PoA Planet accepts an original external content URL as a record for verification. Registration does not create an automatic AE reward.

<Info>
Create Web3 content on an external platform → Copy the original URL → Register the URL on RocX → Verify account ownership and content validity → Publish the verified activity on the PoA Activity Board
</Info>

## Registration eligibility

Users must maintain at least **USD 50 in eligible RocX DeFi deposits** to register an activity. Eligibility is based on the USD value of supported assets, which may include ETH, USDT, USDC, and other assets approved through operating policy.

The initial threshold is USD 50 and may be adjusted through operating policy.

One eligible user can register a maximum of **one link per day**. The daily limit resets according to RocX's service operating timezone as defined in the applicable operating policy.

## Supported external platforms

Initial support may include X, Threads, Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, YouTube, TikTok, Reddit, Medium, Telegram, Mirror, GitHub, and other platforms approved through operating policy.

Supported platforms may expand over time depending on verification capability, metadata accessibility, platform policy, and operational requirements. This list does not claim permanent support or completed API integration for every platform.

## Original URL only

The value submitted by the user is the original content URL. Users cannot author or upload a title, body, description, image, video, file, custom thumbnail, hashtag, or comment inside RocX.

Where available and safe, RocX may retrieve:

- original platform;
- original author or channel;
- original content title and excerpt;
- publication time;
- thumbnail;
- video duration;
- content type; and
- original URL.

Displayed titles, images, and thumbnails are source metadata, not user-authored RocX fields. If metadata cannot be retrieved safely, RocX may display only the platform, author, registration time, original URL, and a **View Original Activity** action. RocX does not claim ownership of or directly host the external content.

## Contributor verification

Users connect supported external accounts in My Page or account settings to their RocX account and wallet. Personal contribution requires the external content's original author or channel to match a connected account.

```text
RocX wallet account
→ Connected social account
→ Submitted external URL
→ Original author or channel verification
→ Contributor ownership confirmed
```

Verification may compare the connected RocX user ID, connected wallet, connected platform account, original author or channel, author identifier in the URL metadata, and the match between registrant and author.

Content from an unconnected account is not recognized as the registrant's own contribution. Users cannot register another person's content as personal contribution. Platforms that cannot expose a reliable original author may be restricted or placed under review.
