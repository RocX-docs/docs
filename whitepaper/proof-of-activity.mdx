Proof of Activity is the shared verification system that connects onchain capital activity with verified user participation inside RocX.

It turns meaningful activity into signals that can contribute to Active Energy, Reputation, Activity Identity, contributor recognition, ecosystem access, partner opportunities, Explore participation, and better DeFi experiences.

<Info>
Proof of Activity measures more than action.

It verifies whether participation can become trusted value.
</Info>

---

## Activity is not the same as proof.

A transaction, link, click, or social action can happen without creating lasting value.

Proof of Activity checks whether activity is authentic, relevant, valid, unique, and meaningful enough to become part of the RocX value layer.

Registration creates a record for review. It does not guarantee verification or reward.

## Not every activity has the same value.

Proof of Activity does not treat every click, follow, post, or transaction equally.

Verification, originality, Web3 relevance, contribution quality, consistency, Reputation, policy compliance, and abuse signals can all affect how activity is interpreted.

Follow verification may remain one supported activity type inside a partner campaign or verified mission. It does not define the PoA model.

---

## One standard connects many sources.

Web3 activity can happen across financial protocols, applications, communities, and external content platforms.

Proof of Activity connects supported activity through a common verification and value framework without claiming ownership of the original content.

The exact verification methods, supported sources, state names, and evaluation weights can evolve through product specification and operating policy.

---

## Proof of Activity is the starting point.

Proof of Activity is not the final benefit and Active Energy is not an automatic result of submission.

Qualified activity can move through verification and evaluation before it becomes eligible for Active Energy or contributes to long-term Reputation and Activity Identity.
