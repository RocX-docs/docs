The participation resource of RocX

Active Energy, or AE, is a non-transferable offchain point that represents eligible activity value inside the RocX ecosystem.

It can be earned or used through supported participation experiences, but it is not a token, does not convert to a token at a fixed rate, and does not guarantee future distribution or financial return.

<Info>
Active Energy represents eligible activity value.

Reputation represents long-term trust and contribution.
</Info>

---

## Activity does not create AE automatically.

Registering a link or completing an action creates a record that may require verification and evaluation.

Qualified activity can become eligible for AE when it meets applicable quality, campaign, period, policy, and abuse-review criteria.

The amount and timing can vary. This whitepaper does not set a registration reward, weekly budget, ranking reward, evaluation reward, minimum evaluation count, or maximum distribution.

## Active Energy and Reputation are different.

AE is a usable participation point connected to eligible activity.

Reputation is a non-transferable record of long-term trust, contribution quality, consistency, expertise, policy compliance, and ecosystem participation.

Holding more AE does not automatically create higher Reputation.

---

## Active Energy supports the next experience.

AE can connect users to Explore participation, ecosystem access, contributor recognition, partner experiences, and better DeFi experiences according to product and operating policy.

It is not designed to reward every post, every link, or every reaction equally.

<Warning>
Active Energy is not transferable and is not an immediate claim on a token, cash payment, yield, or guaranteed future benefit.
</Warning>
