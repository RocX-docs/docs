RocX becomes stronger when accountable capital participation and verified contribution create reasons to keep participating.

## Contribution flywheel

```text
External Web3 Contribution
→ Activity Registration
→ Verification
→ Eligible Evaluation
→ Contributor Ranking
→ Active Energy and Reputation
→ More Participation
→ Explore Activity
→ PoA Rewards Pool Growth
→ More Contributor Incentives
→ Stronger Activity Graph
```

Original content remains on its external platform. RocX makes the verified contribution visible, evaluates it through eligible signals, and records the result in contribution, AE, Reputation, and Activity Identity contexts.

## Capital Layer connection

```text
Eligible DeFi Deposit
→ Registration and Evaluation Eligibility
→ Higher Cost of Abuse
→ More Accountable Evaluation
→ Higher-Quality Contribution Signals
```

The USD 50 initial threshold creates eligibility; it does not buy additional evaluations or weighting.

<Info>
1 eligible wallet = 1 evaluation per activity.
</Info>

## Explore connection

Explore creates continued engagement through approved AE missions. For normally settled winning results, 5% of gross result AE enters the Active Energy Reserve Pool and 5% enters the PoA Rewards Pool. The PoA reserve supports the next Contributor Ranking distribution.

## Long-term value

Round scores reset, while valid activity records and Reputation may continue. Repeated, policy-compliant contribution can strengthen the Activity Graph without promising automatic AE for each activity or a financial return from AE.

**Growth begins with accountable participation.**
