AE is a non-transferable offchain participation unit, not fiat currency or a token. It has no fixed cash value, cannot be immediately converted to a token, and has no fixed conversion rate to ROCX. AE does not guarantee payment, yield, token allocation, financial return, or future value.

PoA ranking distributions are not fixed. The PoA Rewards Pool varies with eligible Explore activity and applicable operating policy. The 10,000 AE example and initial ranking bands are illustrative and do not promise a payout.

The initial USD 50 eligibility threshold may be adjusted through operating policy. Meeting the threshold does not guarantee registration approval, valid evaluation, ranking, AE, or Reputation.
