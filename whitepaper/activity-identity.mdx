## Signals that may be included

- wallet activity and supported DeFi deposit or use;
- contribution by external platform and verified activity links;
- activity fields and original-source metadata;
- Helpful, Not Helpful, and net eligible evaluation results;
- Contributor Ranking history;
- AE earning and use history;
- Reputation changes and long-term consistency;
- hidden, invalidated, or policy-violating activity history; and
- supported Explore participation.

One post, deposit, evaluation, ranking round, or AE balance does not define a user.

## Capital and contribution context

The Capital Graph can provide supported wallet, transaction, deposit, and DeFi context. The Contribution Graph can include verified links, platforms, authors, categories, evaluation, validity, Reputation, ranking, and AE history. Together they form the Activity Identity context without turning activity into a transferable financial asset.

## Pseudonymous and privacy-aware

Activity Identity is designed to be pseudonymous and privacy-aware. It may include connected account identifiers and public activity metadata while remaining separate from a user's legal identity by default.

RocX may process wallet addresses, external-platform account identifiers, public content URLs and metadata, and OAuth or platform-authentication information needed for verification. Authentication tokens and credentials should be stored only to the minimum extent required under the applicable security, retention, and privacy rules.

Original public content remains on the external platform. If it is deleted or made private, the RocX activity state may change. Selling user data is not described as PoA Planet's core business model.

<Warning>
Activity Identity is not legal identity proof, a financial credit score, a transferable asset, or a guarantee of trustworthiness.
</Warning>
