A Web3 identity is not limited to what a user holds or which transactions they make.

It also includes what they research, explain, build, share, and contribute across the broader Web3 ecosystem.

<Info>
Wallet Activity

+ Cross-Platform Content Contribution

+ Community Evaluation

+ Consistency

+ Reputation

= Activity Identity
</Info>

---

## A wallet address is not enough.

A wallet can show assets and transactions.

It does not fully explain contribution quality, areas of expertise, consistency, policy compliance, or the context created across external platforms.

Activity Identity connects those records into a non-transferable user-level signal.

---

## Activity Identity includes capital and contribution.

Activity Identity can include wallet activity, deposits and DeFi participation, external content contribution, platform-level contribution history, verified evaluation, areas of expertise, activity consistency, Reputation, and eligible AE earning or usage history.

These signals do not create a transferable asset or an immediately tradable score.

The purpose is to make meaningful participation visible without claiming ownership of external content.

---

## Activity Graph connects the record.

RocX can describe the long-term data relationship as an Activity Graph.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Capital Graph" icon="coins">
    Supported wallet and DeFi activity that provides financial context.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Contribution Graph" icon="diagram-project">
    Verified links, contributors, platforms, categories, timing, validity, eligible evaluations, quality, expertise, Reputation changes, AE history, and consistency.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Together, the Capital Graph and Contribution Graph can form RocX Activity Identity.

The Activity Graph is not described as user data sold for advertising or distributed without limits.

---

## Activity Identity protects context.

Activity Identity does not require users to write private personal information into PoA Planet.

Contributor verification can use connected external accounts or other supported methods, while the exact method remains platform and policy dependent.

The goal is not to reveal an offline identity. It is to show what a user has built through verifiable activity.

---

## Identity develops over time.

One post, one deposit, one evaluation, or one AE balance does not define a user.

Activity Identity becomes stronger as valid capital activity, contribution, evaluation, consistency, and Reputation accumulate over time.

<Info>
Activity Identity is built.

It is not bought, transferred, or assigned once.
</Info>
