title: "PoA Activity Board"
description: "Find verified activity, review contributor ranking, and manage your activity history."
icon: "table-list"
---

Use the Activity Board to find verified external Web3 contribution, filter by platform, sort recent or popular activity, evaluate activities, bookmark useful links, and open the original source.

Contributor Ranking shows the current user ranking, percentile, round score, and reward eligibility. My Activity can show your links, retrieved source metadata, registration time, verification and visibility status, Helpful, Not Helpful, net score, round inclusion, AE distribution history, and Reputation context.

My AE Balance can show current AE, PoA ranking distributions, and other ledger changes.

<Note>
RocX does not host the original content. The Activity Board does not add internal comments, replies, direct messages, or follower relationships. Final status labels and management actions follow the implemented product specification.
</Note>
