## PoA Activity Board

Use the Activity Board to discover verified external Web3 contribution, filter supported platforms, review contribution-based activity, and access My Activity.

The board displays original external links and available source metadata. RocX does not host the original content or add internal comments, direct messages, or follower relationships through this guide.

Detailed product steps and exact status names will follow the implemented product specification.
