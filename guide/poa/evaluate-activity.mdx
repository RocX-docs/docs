## Evaluate Activity

Eligible wallets can use Helpful or Not Helpful to evaluate the informational, educational, or practical value of a registered activity.

One eligible wallet can provide one evaluation per activity, and a larger deposit does not create more evaluations or greater evaluation power. Not Helpful is different from reporting policy-violating content.

<Note>
The exact eligible deposit rule, evaluation-change policy, self-evaluation restriction, and Contribution Score formula remain subject to product and operating policy.
</Note>
