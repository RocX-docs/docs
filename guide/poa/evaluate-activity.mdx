Use **Helpful** when content provides informational, educational, analytical, or practical value to Web3 users. Use **Not Helpful** when that value is low.

Not Helpful is not a Report. Use the separate Report path for scam, phishing, impersonation, malicious content, or policy violations.

## Before you start

Connect your wallet, maintain at least USD 50 in eligible deposits for 24 hours, and pass the real-time deposit check.

## Evaluate

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open an Activity">Review the original source before deciding.</Step>
  <Step title="Choose one evaluation">Select either **Helpful** or **Not Helpful**.</Step>
  <Step title="Sign with your wallet">Every evaluation requires a wallet signature.</Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the result">After submission, the evaluation cannot be canceled or changed.</Step>
</Steps>

## Limits

- Helpful: maximum 10 per day
- Not Helpful: maximum 10 per day
- Same Activity: Helpful **or** Not Helpful once per user, never both
- Self-evaluation: not allowed
- Cancellation or change: not allowed
- Vote weight: one eligible user has one equal evaluation; a larger deposit adds no weight

<Note>
Invalid, reciprocal, related-account, manipulated, or policy-violating evaluations can be excluded from final scoring.
</Note>
