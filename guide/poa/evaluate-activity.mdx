## Prerequisites

Maintain at least USD 50 in eligible RocX DeFi deposits. Deposit size does not increase evaluation count or weight.

## Choose the correct action

- **Helpful:** The activity provides useful Web3 information, education, analysis, insight, or practical value.
- **Not Helpful:** The activity lacks sufficient informational, educational, analytical, or practical value.
- **Report:** Use the separate reporting path for phishing, impersonation, illegal or harmful content, spam, malicious links, or policy violations.

## Limits

- Helpful: maximum 10 per day
- Not Helpful: maximum 10 per day
- Same activity: one evaluation per eligible wallet
- Maximum combined actions: 20 per day across the separate limits

The raw activity score is eligible Helpful minus eligible Not Helpful. Invalid or abusive evaluations may be excluded. Bookmarking is not an evaluation and does not create AE by itself.

<Note>
The daily reset timezone, self-evaluation, changing or canceling a choice, post-evaluation withdrawal, evaluator weighting, and evaluator AE rewards remain subject to product specification and operating policy.
</Note>
