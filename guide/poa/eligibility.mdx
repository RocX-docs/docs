PoA activity registration, Helpful, and Not Helpful require an eligible RocX DeFi deposit.

## Requirements

1. Reach at least **USD 50** in eligible deposits.
2. Maintain that level continuously for **24 hours**.
3. Pass the scheduled eligibility check.
4. Still have at least USD 50 when each PoA action is attempted.

```text
USD 50+ eligible deposit
→ Maintain for 24 hours
→ Scheduled eligibility check
+ Real-time deposit check
→ Registration and evaluation permission
```

## Two checks

The scheduled snapshot runs daily at **00:00 UTC / 09:00 KST** and reviews the eligible amount, USD 50 threshold, 24-hour duration, and permission state. Every registration, Helpful, and Not Helpful action also performs a real-time deposit check.

<Warning>
If eligible deposits fall below USD 50, access can turn off immediately. Returning to USD 50 or more starts a new 24-hour period; the earlier elapsed time does not carry over.
</Warning>

Deposit size above USD 50 does not increase evaluation count or vote weight. The threshold is an access requirement, not a purchase of additional voting power.

<Note>
The PoA eligibility snapshot at 09:00 KST is different from the Deposit AE snapshot at 08:59:59 KST.
</Note>
