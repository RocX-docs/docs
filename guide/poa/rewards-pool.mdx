The PoA Rewards Pool is a separate offchain AE ledger account dedicated to the 14-day Contributor Ranking. It is not a ROCX token pool, cash pool, or pre-funded Prediction reward pool.

Normally settled Explore winning results can allocate 5% to the PoA Rewards Pool when that mission's approved settlement rule includes the shared 10% allocation. Each mission's own payout math remains controlling.

## Initial distribution bands

| Rank band | Users | Pool share | Example from 10,000 AE |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 1-10 | 10 | 30% | 3,000 AE total; 300 AE each |
| 11-30 | 20 | 30% | 3,000 AE total; 150 AE each |
| 31-60 | 30 | 24% | 2,400 AE total; 80 AE each |
| 61-100 | 40 | 16% | 1,600 AE total; 40 AE each |
| **Total** | **100** | **100%** | **10,000 AE** |

The example is not a fixed return. Actual distribution depends on the AE accumulated in the applicable round.

<Note>
A full tie at a rank divides that rank's assigned AE equally without increasing the total PoA Rewards Pool.
</Note>

See [AE Reserve Pools](../active-energy/reserve-pools) for the separate Active Energy Reserve Pool.
