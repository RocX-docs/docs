A supported social account connects your RocX wallet identity to the account that created the external content.

## Why RocX uses OAuth

OAuth can confirm account ownership, platform user ID, and whether the submitted URL's original author matches the connected account. Its role in PoA is contributor ownership verification.

## Connect an account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open My Page">Go to **Connected Accounts**.</Step>
  <Step title="Select a platform">Choose a platform that the current interface marks as available.</Step>
  <Step title="Authorize OAuth">On the platform's authorization screen, review the requested permissions and approve only if you agree.</Step>
  <Step title="Confirm connection">Return to RocX and verify the connected platform account.</Step>
  <Step title="Meet PoA eligibility">Activity registration becomes available only when the social connection and deposit requirements are both satisfied.</Step>
</Steps>

## Platform status

RocX can release platform support gradually through feature flags. Platforms requiring API access, OAuth approval, or app review can appear as **Coming Soon** or **Under Review**. The documentation repository does not contain the current enabled-platform configuration; the current RocX interface is the source for **Supported Now** status.

<Warning>
Disconnecting or changing an external account can affect contributor verification. Never share OAuth credentials, wallet keys, or recovery phrases with support.
</Warning>
