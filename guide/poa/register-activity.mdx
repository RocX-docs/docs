## Register Activity

Register an original Web3 content URL from a supported external platform. Users do not enter a title, body, media file, attachment, or custom thumbnail inside RocX.

RocX can detect the platform, retrieve appropriate source metadata, and review the source, contributor relationship, uniqueness, relevance, validity, and abuse signals.

<Warning>
Registration creates a record for verification. It does not guarantee approval, Active Energy, ranking, or any other benefit.
</Warning>
