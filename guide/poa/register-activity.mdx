Register your own Web3 content by submitting its original URL. Registration requires a wallet signature and does not create an automatic AE reward.

## Before you start

- Connect your wallet.
- Connect the supported external account that authored the content.
- Maintain at least USD 50 in eligible deposits for 24 hours.
- Use a platform currently enabled in RocX.

## Register the URL

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create externally">Publish the original Web3 contribution on the external platform.</Step>
  <Step title="Open Register Activity">In PoA Planet, select **Register Activity**.</Step>
  <Step title="Paste the original URL">Submit the direct source URL. RocX does not accept an internally written title, body, media, file, thumbnail, or comment.</Step>
  <Step title="Verify source and contributor">RocX checks the platform, URL, accessibility, connected author, duplicate URL or external content ID, content validity, Web3 relevance, and abuse signals.</Step>
  <Step title="Sign with your wallet">Review and approve the registration signature.</Step>
  <Step title="Submit">Wait for the verification or publishing status shown in the interface.</Step>
</Steps>

Verification can combine platform APIs and metadata, automated checks, abuse detection, and operational review. It is not described as a guaranteed 100% AI decision.

<Warning>
One user can register a maximum of **one Activity link per day**, or 14 theoretical links in a 14-day round. Deleted, private, inaccessible, author-mismatched, malicious, duplicate, or policy-violating content can be rejected or later invalidated.
</Warning>
