## Prerequisites

- Maintain at least USD 50 in eligible RocX DeFi deposits.
- Connect the external account that originally created the content to your RocX account and wallet.
- Use a supported or operating-policy-approved platform.
- Stay within the maximum of one registered activity link per day.

## Register the original URL

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the content externally">
    Publish your original Web3 contribution on the external platform.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the original URL">
    Use the direct source link, not a duplicate, modified, or unrelated link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit the URL">
    RocX accepts the URL only. You cannot enter a title, body, description, media, file, custom thumbnail, hashtag, or comment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for verification">
    RocX may review the source, connected author, uniqueness, Web3 relevance, accessibility, validity, and abuse signals.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Initial support may include X, Threads, Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, YouTube, TikTok, Reddit, Medium, Telegram, Mirror, GitHub, and other approved platforms. Support may change with verification capability and platform policy.

Pending, Verified, Rejected, Hidden, and Invalidated describe the conceptual activity lifecycle; the product interface may use finalized implementation labels.

<Warning>
One eligible user can register no more than one link per day. Registration or verification does not guarantee AE, Reputation, ranking, or another benefit.
</Warning>
