The visible Activity score is the difference between eligible Helpful and eligible Not Helpful evaluations.

```text
Net Score = Eligible Helpful - Eligible Not Helpful
```

## Example

| Eligible Helpful | Eligible Not Helpful | Net Score |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 500 | 100 | 400 |

## Final ranking review

The final Contributor Ranking can also reflect contributor verification, content validity, duplicate detection, abuse review, reciprocal voting, related-account signals, invalidated content, and policy violations.

<Note>
RocX does not publish or invent a fixed hidden-weight formula. The visible Net Score is simple, while invalid or abusive records can be excluded before final ranking.
</Note>
