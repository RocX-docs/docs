My Activity is the personal view of external URLs you registered through PoA Planet.

## What you can review

Depending on the current MVP interface, a record can show the original URL and platform, retrieved source metadata, registration time, verification or visibility status, Helpful, Not Helpful, Net Score, current-round inclusion, AE distribution, and Reputation context.

## Status changes

An Activity can be reviewed again when its original content becomes deleted, private, unavailable, author-mismatched, malicious, duplicate, manipulated, or policy-violating. A status change can remove the Activity and its evaluations from final ranking.

## Related history

Use [Activity History](../profile/activity-history) for the broader account view and [AE History](../active-energy/ae-history) for ledger changes.
