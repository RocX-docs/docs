PoA Planet is RocX's cross-platform Participation & Contribution Layer.

<Info>
Create anywhere. Prove it on RocX.
</Info>

Users create Web3 content on an external platform and register the original URL in RocX. RocX does not host the original content. Only information required for verification and contribution records may be processed, such as platform, author, title, excerpt, publication time, thumbnail, content type, and URL.

## How it works

```text
Create externally
→ Register original URL
→ Verify contributor and content
→ Eligible community evaluation
→ 14-day Contributor Ranking
→ AE distribution
→ Reputation and Activity Identity
```

## What PoA is not

RocX is not a new social network. Users do not compose a title, body, description, image, video, file, thumbnail, or comment inside the PoA registration flow. Registration is based on the original external content URL.
