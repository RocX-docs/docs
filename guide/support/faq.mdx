## What is RocX?

RocX is a Proof of Activity-powered DeFi platform that combines onchain capital activity with verified user participation.

## Is RocX currently on mainnet?

No mainnet status is claimed in this guide. It describes the August 2026 MVP/testnet environment; rely on an official RocX launch notice for mainnet.

## What is Active Energy?

AE is a non-transferable offchain participation unit with no fixed cash value.

## Is AE a token? Can it be converted directly to ROCX?

No. AE is not a token and has no fixed AE-to-ROCX conversion rate.

## How do I earn AE?

Current paths include Deposit AE, a successful daily Attendance proof, finalized PoA Contributor Ranking distribution, and mission-specific Explore results. Not every activity automatically earns AE.

## Why do I need a USD 50 deposit for PoA?

It is the access requirement for Activity registration, Helpful, and Not Helpful. Depositing more does not increase evaluation weight.

## Why do I need to wait 24 hours?

The eligible amount must remain at or above USD 50 continuously for 24 hours before permission can activate.

## How many Activity links can I register per day?

One original link per user per day.

## Can I evaluate my own Activity?

No. Self-evaluation is prohibited.

## Can I change or cancel Helpful or Not Helpful?

No. Choose one per Activity; a completed evaluation cannot be changed or canceled.

## How many evaluations can I make per day?

Up to 10 Helpful and 10 Not Helpful, with one evaluation total per Activity.

## Why do I need to sign when registering or evaluating?

The wallet signature authorizes the action from the connected account. Review each request before signing.

## What happens if my deposit falls below USD 50?

Real-time verification can disable registration and evaluation immediately. Returning to USD 50 starts a new 24-hour qualification period.

## How does the 14-day ranking work?

A round begins every other Sunday at 00:00 UTC / 09:00 KST. Eligible Activity scores are aggregated by contributor, revalidated, reviewed, and ranked. The finalized top 100 can receive the round's PoA Rewards Pool AE.

## What happens if my original post is deleted?

It is excluded from final ranking, and its evaluations do not count. Private, unavailable, author-mismatched, malicious, duplicate, or policy-violating content can also be invalidated.

## Where do PoA rewards come from?

They come from the separate PoA Rewards Pool, including applicable 5% allocations from normally settled Explore winning results under mission-specific rules.

## What is the difference between Total AE Pool and PoA Rewards Pool?

Total AE Pool is a user-facing alias for the common Active Energy Reserve Pool. PoA Rewards Pool is separate and funds Contributor Ranking distributions.

## Does playing Explore count as Daily Attendance?

No. Open Attendance, select **Start Proof of Activity**, and sign with your wallet.

## How much AE do I get for Attendance?

A successful daily check-in records 100 AE. The Streak Boost does not increase this 100 AE.

## How does Attendance Streak affect Deposit AE?

It adds 0% on Days 1-6, 5% on Days 7-14, 10% on Days 15-29, and 15% on Day 30+. Missing a day restarts the next Attendance at Day 1.

## How does Card Flip work?

Choose a 100-1,000 AE amount and select one of nine cards. Only the selected card reveals a 250%, 150%, KEEP, -20%, or -50% result under the documented composition.

## Why is only my selected Card Flip card revealed?

That is the MVP v2 UI rule. The other eight cards remain hidden, including after refresh or reconnect.

## What happens if a Roulette room does not fill?

The timer starts with the first participant. If the room remains incomplete for 24 hours, the round is canceled and entry AE is refunded.

## What happens if a game has a server error?

If a server, network, or required-data error prevents a valid result, the game is canceled and refunded 100%. A normal losing result is not an error.

## What is Health Factor?

It is a collateral-versus-debt safety indicator. A lower value generally means higher liquidation risk.

## Can I lose testnet assets?

Yes. Testnet tokens lack mainnet economic value but can still be lost, reset, become inaccessible, or be affected by contract, bridge, oracle, wallet, or network failures.

## Are Swap and Bridge mainnet services?

This guide describes only the implemented MVP/testnet flows. It does not claim mainnet availability or name an unverified provider.
