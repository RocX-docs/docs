Current policies can record AE through several separate paths.

| Source | Current rule |
|---|---|
| Deposit AE | 0.5 AE per eligible USD per day at the daily snapshot |
| Attendance | 100 AE after a successful daily **Start Proof of Activity** wallet signature |
| PoA | Distribution from the round's PoA Rewards Pool to the finalized top 100 Contributor Ranking |
| Explore | Mission-specific settlement results |

Registration, verification, a single Helpful evaluation, bookmarking, or game participation alone does not guarantee AE.

## Boosts

Attendance Streak Boost and eligible NFT Profile Boost can add together for Deposit AE, up to a total **+30% cap**. The current NFT mapping must be read from the implemented product; this repository does not define collection or tier names.

<Note>
Each AE source has its own eligibility, timing, settlement, and abuse-review rules. Policy is subject to change before mainnet.
</Note>
