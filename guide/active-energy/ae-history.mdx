AE History is the account view for offchain AE ledger changes.

## What a record can show

- Date and time
- Source or mission
- Added or reduced AE
- Claim, refund, or settlement status
- Related round, game, or Activity reference when available
- Balance after the ledger change

## Check an unexpected balance

1. Compare Attendance, Deposit AE snapshot, PoA distribution, and Explore histories separately.
2. Check whether a claim or refund is still pending.
3. Confirm the displayed wallet account.
4. Record the timestamp, amount, status, and related transaction or game identifier before contacting support.

<Note>
AE History is an offchain participation ledger, not a token transaction history.
</Note>
