Daily Attendance is completed only from the Attendance page. Playing Roulette, Card Flip, or Prediction does not complete Attendance.

## Complete Attendance

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Attendance">Go to the Attendance page in Explore Planet.</Step>
  <Step title="Start the proof">Select **Start Proof of Activity**.</Step>
  <Step title="Sign with your wallet">Review and approve the message signature.</Step>
  <Step title="Confirm success">A successful daily check-in records **100 AE**.</Step>
</Steps>

The 100 AE daily reward does not receive a Streak Boost.

## Attendance Streak

| Current streak | Deposit AE Boost |
|---|---:|
| Day 1-6 | 0% |
| Day 7-14 | +5% |
| Day 15-29 | +10% |
| Day 30+ | +15% |

Missing a day means the next successful Attendance starts again at Day 1. Withdrawal does not reset the Attendance Streak. Partial withdrawal recalculates the eligible deposited amount; full withdrawal leaves no Deposit AE. A later deposit uses the current streak tier if that streak still exists.

<Note>
Attendance Boost applies to Deposit AE, not to the daily 100 AE check-in reward.
</Note>
