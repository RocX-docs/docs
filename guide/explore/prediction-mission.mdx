description: "AE settlement principles for the RocX Prediction mission."
icon: "chart-line"

Prediction is an Explore participation mission governed by its product-specific market, result, cancellation, refund, draw, failed-settlement, and abuse rules.

For a normally settled winning result, the user receives 90% of gross result AE, 5% enters the Active Energy Reserve Pool, and 5% enters the PoA Rewards Pool.

<Note>
Prediction does not require a separately pre-funded pool in this whitepaper. The PoA Rewards Pool funds Contributor Ranking distributions, not the Prediction win itself.
</Note>
