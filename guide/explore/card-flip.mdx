title: "Card Flip Mission"
description: "AE settlement principles for the RocX Card Flip mission."
icon: "cards"
---

Card Flip is an Explore participation mission governed by its product-specific entry, result, cancellation, refund, and abuse rules.

For a normally settled winning result, the user receives 90% of gross result AE, 5% enters the Active Energy Reserve Pool, and 5% enters the PoA Rewards Pool.

<Note>
Card Flip does not require a separately pre-funded pool in this whitepaper. The PoA Rewards Pool funds Contributor Ranking distributions, not the Card Flip win itself.
</Note>
