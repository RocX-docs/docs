Card Flip displays nine card backs. Select one card; only that card flips and reveals its result. The other eight stay hidden, including after refresh or reconnect.

## Rules

- Bet: minimum 100 AE; maximum 1,000 AE
- Each card slot: 1/9, or 11.11%
- Composition: one 250%, one 150%, one KEEP, three -20%, three -50%

| Result | Overall probability | Result for 100 AE |
|---|---:|---:|
| 250% | 11.11% | 250 AE gross; 10% claim deduction; **225 AE final** |
| 150% | 11.11% | 150 AE gross; 10% claim deduction; **135 AE final** |
| KEEP | 11.11% | **100 AE returned** |
| -20% | 33.33% | **80 AE returned** |
| -50% | 33.33% | **50 AE returned** |

User RTP is **94.44%**. The overall result rate before claim deduction is **98.89%**, and average user AE reduction is **5.56%**. These figures explain game rules, not investment returns.

## Play

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter an amount">Choose 100-1,000 AE.</Step>
  <Step title="Select one card">Choose one of the nine backs.</Step>
  <Step title="Reveal the selected result">Only your selected card flips; the other eight remain hidden.</Step>
  <Step title="Settle">Review the final amount and status in Game History.</Step>
</Steps>

The 10% claim deduction on the 250% and 150% results is split 5% to the Active Energy Reserve Pool and 5% to the PoA Rewards Pool.

<Note>
Game rules are fixed when a session begins. Later rule updates apply only to newly created games.
</Note>
