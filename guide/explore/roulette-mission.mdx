---
title: "Roulette Mission"
description: "AE settlement principles for the RocX Roulette mission."
icon: "circle-notch"
---

Roulette is an Explore participation mission governed by its product-specific entry, result, cancellation, refund, and abuse rules.

For a normally settled winning result, the user receives 90% of gross result AE, 5% enters the Active Energy Reserve Pool, and 5% enters the PoA Rewards Pool.

<Note>
The PoA Rewards Pool funds Contributor Ranking distributions, not the Roulette win itself. Cancellation, refund, draw, and failed-settlement treatment follows the mission specification.
</Note>
