Roulette offers three rooms that can be open at the same time: **4 Player A**, **4 Player B**, and **8 Player C**. Entry is fixed at **100 AE**.

## Join a round

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a room">Select one available round. One wallet can hold one seat in a round.</Step>
  <Step title="Confirm entry">Approve the 100 AE entry action.</Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the room">The empty-round timer starts with the first participant. The last participant fills the room and triggers the spin immediately.</Step>
  <Step title="Review the result">The animation must stop on the winner segment selected by the server.</Step>
  <Step title="Claim if you win">The winner uses a wallet signature to claim. MVP claims do not expire.</Step>
</Steps>

There is no Ready button or 30-second Ready delay. The same wallet can participate in up to three different rounds at once.

## Cancellation and pool allocation

If a room does not fill within 24 hours of its first participant, the round is canceled and entry AE is refunded. A server, network, or transient error that prevents a valid result also cancels the game with a 100% refund.

When the approved Roulette payout includes the shared 10% allocation, 5% is recorded in the Active Energy Reserve Pool and 5% in the PoA Rewards Pool. The winner amount uses the current Roulette-specific rule shown in the product; this repository does not contain a separate payout table.
