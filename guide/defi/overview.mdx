DeFi Planet is RocX's Capital Layer. The current guide covers Deposit, Withdraw, Borrow, Repay, Health Factor, Swap, and Bridge flows shown in the MVP.

<Info>
These steps use testnet assets. Testnet balances are not equivalent to real assets, and product rules or smart contracts may change before mainnet.
</Info>

## Available workflows

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Deposit" icon="vault" href="./deposit">Supply a supported asset and review Deposit AE eligibility.</Card>
  <Card title="Withdraw" icon="arrow-up-from-bracket" href="./withdraw">Return an available supplied amount to your wallet.</Card>
  <Card title="Borrow" icon="hand-holding-dollar" href="./borrow">Borrow against supported collateral and monitor risk.</Card>
  <Card title="Repay" icon="rotate-left" href="./repay">Reduce or close debt using the borrowed asset.</Card>
  <Card title="Health Factor" icon="heart-pulse" href="./health-factor">Understand collateral safety and liquidation risk.</Card>
  <Card title="Swap" icon="arrows-rotate" href="./swap">Exchange supported assets through the implemented testnet route.</Card>
  <Card title="Bridge" icon="bridge" href="./bridge">Move a supported asset between displayed networks.</Card>
  <Card title="Safety" icon="shield-halved" href="./safety">Review testnet, wallet, approval, and transaction risks.</Card>
</CardGroup>

<Warning>
A successful transaction can still change your collateral position. Review asset, amount, network, approval, and Health Factor before confirming.
</Warning>
