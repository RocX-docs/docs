Withdraw returns an available supplied asset from DeFi Planet to your connected wallet.

## Before you start

Review **Available to Withdraw**, current debt, collateral use, and the Health Factor preview. A supplied balance can be larger than the amount safely available.

## Withdraw

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select the asset">Open the supplied position and choose **Withdraw**.</Step>
  <Step title="Choose partial or full">Enter an amount no greater than **Available to Withdraw**. A full withdrawal may be unavailable while debt depends on the collateral.</Step>
  <Step title="Review risk">Check the projected Health Factor and any liquidation warning.</Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">Approve the transaction in your wallet and wait for its final status.</Step>
  <Step title="Check balances">Confirm the wallet balance and remaining supplied position.</Step>
</Steps>

## Effects outside DeFi

- Deposit AE is recalculated from the eligible deposited amount.
- Attendance Streak is not reset by a partial or full withdrawal.
- A full withdrawal leaves no eligible amount for Deposit AE.
- If eligible deposits fall below USD 50, PoA registration and evaluation access can turn off immediately after the real-time check.
- Returning to USD 50 or more starts a new 24-hour qualification period.

<Warning>
Withdrawing collateral can lower Health Factor and increase liquidation risk. Repay debt or reduce the withdrawal if the preview is unsafe.
</Warning>
