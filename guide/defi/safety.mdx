RocX is currently in an MVP/testnet phase. Smart contract security review and external audit are part of the pre-mainnet process.

<Warning>
This guide does not claim that RocX has completed a CertiK audit or any other final mainnet audit. No audited badge, mainnet contract, RPC address, or security guarantee should be inferred from the testnet interface.
</Warning>

## Before confirming

- Verify the official domain, connected wallet, network, asset, and amount.
- Read approval scopes and revoke approvals you no longer need using a trusted tool.
- Treat Borrow and Withdraw previews as risk-sensitive actions.
- Confirm both source and destination details for Bridge.
- Keep transaction hashes when requesting support.

## Testnet risk

Testnet assets can still be lost, become inaccessible, fail to settle, or be reset. Smart contracts, oracles, routers, bridges, wallets, RPC services, and frontends can fail. Never use a seed phrase or private key in a website or support chat.

See [Security & Wallet Safety](../support/security) for account-level practices.
