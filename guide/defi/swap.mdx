Swap exchanges one supported asset for another. RocX documentation does not identify the current routing provider, so this page does not describe RocX as operating its own AMM.

## Swap an asset

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select From">Choose the asset you want to exchange.</Step>
  <Step title="Select To">Choose the asset you want to receive and enter the amount.</Step>
  <Step title="Review the quote">Check the quoted rate and, when displayed, price impact, slippage, minimum received, route, and network fee.</Step>
  <Step title="Approve when required">Confirm the token approval before the Swap transaction.</Step>
  <Step title="Confirm Swap">Review the final quote, sign the transaction, and wait for confirmation.</Step>
  <Step title="Check the balance">Verify the received asset after settlement.</Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Quotes can expire or change before confirmation. Refresh the quote instead of approving unexpected output. No default slippage value is documented because it is not present in this repository.
</Note>
