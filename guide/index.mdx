RocX is a Proof of Activity-powered DeFi platform that combines onchain capital activity with verified user participation.

<Info>
This guide describes the RocX MVP in a testnet environment as of August 2026. Testnet assets do not have the same economic value as real assets. Product policy and smart contracts may change before mainnet; rely on official launch notices when mainnet becomes available.
</Info>
