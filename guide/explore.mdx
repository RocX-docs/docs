Explore Planet is RocX's AE-based Engagement Layer. Attendance, Roulette, Card Flip, and Prediction have different participation and settlement rules.

<Info>
AE is not fiat currency or a token. Explore runs in the MVP/testnet environment. Review the rule shown for each mission before participating.
</Info>

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Attendance" icon="calendar-check" href="./explore/activity-proof">Sign a separate daily proof to receive 100 AE.</Card>
  <Card title="Roulette" icon="circle-notch" href="./explore/roulette-mission">Join a 4-player or 8-player room with a fixed 100 AE entry.</Card>
  <Card title="Card Flip" icon="cards" href="./explore/card-flip">Select one of nine hidden cards under MVP v2 rules.</Card>
  <Card title="Prediction" icon="chart-line" href="./explore/prediction-mission">Choose BTC/USDT UP or DOWN for an individual 60-second round.</Card>
</CardGroup>

## Settlement principles

The server result is final. A technical cancellation caused by a server, network, or required-data error receives a 100% refund under the applicable rule. A normally settled losing result is not a technical failure and is handled under the mission rule. Game History should display actual settlement status, latest first.

<Warning>
Do not refresh or repeat a request while a result or claim is pending. Check Game History first.
</Warning>
