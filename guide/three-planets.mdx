RocX organizes the MVP into three product areas with different roles.

| Planet | Layer | What you use it for |
|---|---|---|
| **DeFi Planet** | Capital Layer | Deposit, Withdraw, Borrow, Repay, Swap, and Bridge on supported testnet flows |
| **PoA Planet** | Participation & Contribution Layer | Connect an external account, register an original Web3 content URL, evaluate eligible activity, and join Contributor Ranking |
| **Explore Planet** | Engagement Layer | Complete Attendance and use AE in Roulette, Card Flip, and Prediction under mission-specific rules |

<Info>
DeFi Planet measures capital. PoA Planet measures contribution. Explore Planet keeps participation moving.
</Info>

## A typical path

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect">Connect a supported wallet on the network shown by RocX.</Step>
  <Step title="Use DeFi Planet">Deposit an eligible asset if you want to build Deposit AE or qualify for PoA.</Step>
  <Step title="Contribute through PoA">Connect a supported external account and register your original content URL after meeting eligibility.</Step>
  <Step title="Keep participating">Complete Attendance separately and review each Explore mission before using AE.</Step>
</Steps>

RocX is not a new social network and does not host the original content registered through PoA Planet.
