RocX can display more than one ranking context. Do not assume every leaderboard uses the same score.

## PoA Contributor Ranking

The PoA ranking aggregates eligible contribution over a 14-day round, applies final verification and abuse review, and uses Net Score tie-breaking. See [14-Day Contributor Ranking](../poa/contributor-ranking).

## Other leaderboards

An Explore, Activity, or general leaderboard uses the metric and period shown in that tab. The active tabs and formulas are not present in this repository, so this guide does not claim a universal formula.

<Note>
Contributor Ranking is the official current term for PoA.
</Note>
