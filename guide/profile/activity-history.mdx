Activity History can combine links to separate PoA and Explore records for the connected wallet.

## PoA records

Review registered URLs, source metadata, verification status, Helpful, Not Helpful, Net Score, round inclusion, and finalized distribution status in [My Activity](../poa/my-activity).

## Explore records

Review mission, entry amount, server result, settlement, claim, cancellation, or refund status. Normal losses and technical cancellations are different outcomes.

## Attendance records

Attendance is a separate daily proof. A Roulette, Card Flip, or Prediction record does not prove daily Attendance.

<Note>
Long-term Activity History can remain after a new 14-day Contributor Ranking begins, even though the round score restarts.
</Note>
