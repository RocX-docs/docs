RocX is a Proof of Activity-powered DeFi platform that combines onchain capital activity with verified user participation.

Proof of Activity does not replace DeFi.

It adds a Participation and Contribution Layer that connects capital, cross-platform contribution, Active Energy, Reputation, and Activity Identity through one measurable value framework.

<Info>
DeFi Planet measures capital.

PoA Planet measures contribution.

Explore Planet keeps participation moving.
</Info>

Verified contribution can support Active Energy, Reputation, Activity Identity, contributor recognition, partner opportunities, Explore participation, ecosystem access, and better DeFi experiences.

DeFi remains the core financial layer, but it is not the only destination of PoA value.
