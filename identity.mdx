Activity Identity connects wallet activity, cross-platform content contribution, eligible community evaluation, consistency, and Reputation into a non-transferable user-level signal.

A Web3 identity is not limited to assets or transactions. It can also reflect what users research, explain, build, share, and contribute across supported external platforms.

Activity Identity can include DeFi participation, verified links, platform history, areas of expertise, valid evaluation, consistency, Reputation, and eligible AE earning or usage history.

It is not a transferable asset, an immediately tradable score, or a guarantee of financial access.

Contributor verification can use connected external accounts or other supported methods without requiring users to publish sensitive personal information inside PoA Planet.
